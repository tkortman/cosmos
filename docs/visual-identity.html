Visual Identity

Our visual system — logo, color, type, photography, icons, and illustration — working together to create a cohesive brand.

Visual Identity Overview

Our visual system is built to be recognizable, flexible, and joyful. Every element — from our logo to our illustration style — works together to create a cohesive brand experience across all touchpoints.

Logo

The Shipt logo is our most recognizable brand asset. It should always appear crisp, legible, and with adequate clear space.

Primary Logo

[Primary logo — upload asset]

Logo Variations

Use the appropriate variant depending on background and context:

  • Full color — Default for light backgrounds
  • Reversed (white) — For dark or photographic backgrounds
  • Monochrome — For single-color applications
[Logo variations grid — upload asset]

Clear Space & Minimum Size

Maintain a minimum clear space equal to the height of the "S" in Shipt on all sides. Never display the logo smaller than 80px wide in digital or 0.75" in print.

Accessibility

All color pairings must meet WCAG 2.1 AA contrast requirements (minimum 4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large text).

Typography

Type is one of our most powerful brand tools. Our typeface system balances personality with readability.

Primary Typeface

Euclid Circular A is our primary brand typeface. Use it for headlines, body copy, and UI elements.

[Typography specimen — upload asset]

Type Scale & Hierarchy

  • Display: 48–72px / Bold
  • H1: 36px / Bold
  • H2: 24px / Semibold
  • H3: 18px / Semibold
  • Body: 15–16px / Regular
  • Caption: 12–13px / Regular

Photography

Photography is central to how we communicate warmth and authenticity. Images should feel candid, joyful, and representative of the diverse communities we serve.

Visual Tone

  • Natural lighting, warm tones
  • Real people in real moments
  • Diverse representation across age, ethnicity, ability, and family structure
  • Product shots should feel approachable, not sterile

Illustration

Our illustration style extends our brand personality into areas where photography isn't practical. Illustrations should feel energetic, inclusive, and aligned with our color palette.

Style Guidelines

  • Rounded shapes and soft edges
  • Limited color palette per illustration (3–5 colors max)
  • Consistent stroke weight when line-based
  • Characters should reflect diversity and warmth
