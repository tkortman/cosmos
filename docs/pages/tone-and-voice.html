Tone & Voice

How Shipt sounds across every channel — our writing principles, messaging hierarchy, and tagline guidelines.

Tone & Voice

Shipt's voice is a direct extension of our brand personality. We speak like a knowledgeable friend — someone who genuinely wants to help and knows how to make your day a little better.

Writing Principles

  1. Lead with value — Tell people what they gain, not what we do.
  2. Be specific — Concrete details build trust. Avoid vague superlatives.
  3. Keep it conversational — Write like you speak. If it sounds stiff read aloud, rewrite it.
  4. Respect their time — Say more with fewer words. Every sentence should earn its place.

Messaging Framework

Use the following hierarchy when crafting communications:

  1. Headline: A single, benefit-driven statement.
  2. Supporting Copy: 1–2 sentences that add context or specificity.
  3. CTA: Clear, action-oriented. Use verbs ("Shop now," "Get started").

Tagline Usage

"No Order is Ordinary"

This is our primary tagline. It communicates that every delivery is personal and intentional. Use it in headline positions, paired with supporting context.

  • Always use title case
  • Never alter or abbreviate the tagline
  • May appear alongside the Shipt logo with minimum clear space respected
