Brand campaigns, key messaging platforms, and content guidelines for Shipt's storytelling.

Totemeal

Totemeal is a brand concept representing the complete meal experience — from planning to delivery to the table. It encompasses the idea that every ingredient, every choice, and every delivery is part of a larger story.

Usage Context

Use "Totemeal" in campaign work focused on meal solutions, family dinners, and recipe-driven content. Always capitalize as a proper noun.

No Order is Ordinary

This campaign platform celebrates the personal care Shipt shoppers bring to every order. It's the emotional heart of our brand narrative.

Key Messages

  • Every order is handpicked by a real person who cares
  • Small details matter — from selecting the ripest avocado to remembering preferences
  • Delivery is personal, not transactional

Same-Day Delivery

Same-Day Delivery is a core service differentiator. When communicating this benefit, always pair the speed message with our personal touch narrative.

Messaging Guidelines

  • Lead with the benefit: "Get it today"
  • Follow with the personal angle: "Handpicked by your Shipt shopper"
  • Never promise specific delivery windows in brand creative without legal review

All same-day delivery claims must include the disclaimer: "Same-day delivery subject to availability. Delivery windows vary by location and order time." See Legal & Compliance section for full requirements.

Seasonal Campaigns

Seasonal moments (holidays, back-to-school, summer) are opportunities to show how Shipt fits into life's rhythms. Seasonal creative should feel timely but always on-brand.

  • Maintain brand color palette as foundation — seasonal accents are additive, not replacements
  • Photography should reflect the season without clichés
  • Tagline usage remains consistent year-round
