Legal & Compliance

Mandatory disclaimers, regulatory requirements, and compliance guidelines for all Shipt brand communications.

Legal Disclaimers

All marketing materials must comply with applicable legal requirements. The following disclaimers are mandatory when their conditions apply.

Disclaimer text must always be legible, meeting minimum size requirements: 8pt in print, 11px in digital. Disclaimers must not be obscured by imagery, color, or placement.

Alcohol Guidelines

Alcohol delivery is subject to strict legal and regulatory requirements that vary by state and municipality.

Required disclaimer for all alcohol-related communications: "Must be 21+ to order alcohol. Delivery available only in select areas. Valid ID required at delivery. Shipt Shoppers are independent contractors and are required to verify age and sobriety before completing delivery. Drink responsibly."

Creative Guidelines

  • Never depict minors in alcohol-related creative
  • Avoid imagery that associates alcohol with driving, athletic performance, or social pressure
  • All models must appear clearly over 25 years of age
  • Do not use language that encourages excessive consumption

Pricing Disclosures

Pricing claims require careful treatment to ensure accuracy and compliance.

Required disclaimer for pricing communications: "Prices shown are subject to change without notice. In-app prices may differ from in-store prices. Membership and service fees may apply. See app for details and current pricing in your area."

Best Practices

  • Never guarantee specific price points in creative without legal approval
  • Always clarify whether prices include delivery/service fees
  • Promotional pricing must include expiration dates and limitations
