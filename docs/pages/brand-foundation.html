Philosophy

Philosophy

Single system, diverse expressions — how our brand adapts across every channel while staying unmistakably Shipt.

01 — Single System, Diverse Expressions

This guide is our single source of truth. Every asset, campaign, email, and social post is born from the same visual and verbal DNA outlined in these pages.

However, brand consistency does not mean identical execution. We do not believe in "matching luggage." Great enterprise brands honor the environment in which their audience meets them.

02 — Channel-Native Strategy

Every channel has a specific purpose, and no single execution can do it all.

Some prioritize storytelling, emotional connection, and cultural relevance to meet consumers where they are.

Some prioritize speed and utility to ensure seamless transactions.

The strength of our brand lies in recognizing the distinction. While all executions share a core identity, their expression adapts based on the channel's role and the audience's mindset.

03 — How to Use This Guide

Think of these guidelines as a set of master instruments, not a rigid mold. Use them to construct experiences that feel unmistakably Shipt, anchored in our tone, colors, and human-centric sentiment.

When we respect both our core brand standards and the native context of each medium, we build a brand that is both emotionally captivating and frictionlessly functional.
