Vision

Shipt delivers a personal touch in every interaction. We believe that the way people get their essentials should feel as good as what they're getting. Our promise is to bring joy, ease, and reliability to everyday life.

Our brand promise underpins every piece of communication — from push notifications to billboard campaigns.

Brand Personality

  • Helpful but never overbearing
  • Warm but not saccharine
  • Confident but not arrogant
  • Playful but purposeful

These traits inform our tone across all channels and should remain consistent regardless of audience segment.
